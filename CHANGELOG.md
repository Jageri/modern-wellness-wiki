# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-09-17

### Added

- A complete bilingual library of 100 Chinese and 100 English longevity entries.
- Per-entry audit records with `AUDIT.md`, `PMID_VERIFICATION.md`, and `HANDOFF.md` for all 100 entries.
- Housing, travel, wealth, food-safety, aging-trajectory, swimming, sleep-environment, collagen, and supplement-evaluation topics.
- A risk-based second-pass spot check and a dedicated second-pass gate for every remaining numeric mortality prefix.

### Changed

- Reorganized the Chinese and English taxonomies and rebuilt the main index.
- Reassessed numeric filename prefixes against direct all-cause mortality or lifespan evidence; uncertain, indirect, or non-generalizable effects now use `~`.
- Rewrote entries to distinguish association from causation, relative from absolute risk, human from animal evidence, and hard outcomes from surrogate endpoints.
- Synchronized bilingual claims, limitations, references, and PubMed identifiers.
- Updated hearing-protection evidence, caloric-restriction participant and conflict-of-interest details, sugary-drink limitations, and Chinese insurance definitions.

### Removed

- Obsolete duplicate paths, legacy audit locations, and unsupported or overconfident mortality labels.
